OUR PRIVACY COMMITMENT

Privacy Is Built Into Everything We Do

SupportSphere is committed to handling personal and business information responsibly. We collect only the information necessary to deliver our services, communicate effectively, and improve client experiences.

We believe privacy is built on transparency. Our practices are designed to ensure that information is processed fairly, protected appropriately, and used only for legitimate business purposes.

Whether you're a client, partner, or website visitor, we strive to maintain your trust through responsible data management, secure operations, and clear communication about how information is handled.

INFORMATION HANDLING

How We Handle Information

We are committed to managing information responsibly throughout its lifecycle. Every stage is guided by transparency, security, and respect for your privacy.

Information Collection

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services, respond to inquiries, and improve the overall client experience.

Data Usage

Information is used solely for legitimate business purposes, including service delivery, communication, operational improvements, and legal obligations where applicable.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill business, contractual, or legal requirements before being securely removed or archived.

Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to enhance functionality, understand visitor behavior, and improve the browsing experience.

PRIVACY & SECURITY PRACTICES

Protecting Information Every Step of the Way

Our privacy program combines responsible information handling with operational safeguards to help protect client and business data throughout every engagement.

Secure Infrastructure

Business systems are protected through secure technologies, controlled environments, and ongoing operational oversight.

Access Controls

Information is accessible only to authorized personnel with access granted according to business responsibilities.

Data Protection

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are implemented to help protect information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Employee Confidentiality

Team members are expected to follow confidentiality obligations and responsible information handling practices.

Third-Party Services

Trusted third-party providers may support certain business functions while maintaining appropriate privacy and security standards.

Compliance

Our operational practices are regularly reviewed to support applicable legal, contractual, and business requirements.

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You're Always in Control of Your Information

We believe individuals should have clear visibility into how their information is handled. Depending on your relationship with SupportSphere and applicable laws, you may exercise certain privacy rights regarding your personal information.

  • Request access to information we maintain
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally permitted
  • Update your communication preferences
  • Contact us regarding privacy-related concerns

Access
